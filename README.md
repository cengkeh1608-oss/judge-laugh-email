# Make the Judge Laugh in One Line

One funny line, 200 characters or fewer, on any topic. The entry program
writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`; a judge model then reads the lines
two at a time and picks the funnier one.

The line: "The agent summarised the meeting in one line: 'Nobody knew why
we were there, including the person who called it.'"

Why this line: it reads like a real meeting summary, the joke lands in the
final clause, and every word earns its place — no setup, no explanation.
